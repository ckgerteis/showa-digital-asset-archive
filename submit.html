Submit a Digital Asset

Help us grow the archive by submitting links to high-quality 3D models or datasets.

Before You Submit

  • Relevance: Ensure the asset dates between 1926 and 1989 (Shōwa Era).
  • Licence: Listings under any licence may be submitted, open or commercial. Give the licence exactly as the listing states it. The archive links to listings and never redistributes files.
  • Platform: Sketchfab, Fab, CGTrader, the Unity Asset Store, or an institutional repository. Link to the listing page itself, not to a search result or a download.
  • Accuracy: Give a brief historical context if known, and say what might be anachronistic. Every submission is checked against its listing before it enters the catalog.
